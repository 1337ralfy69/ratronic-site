A proper dashboard display for the VW T3

I built this for my own van because I wanted to see what the engine was doing without a laptop on the passenger seat. It turned out well enough that friends wanted one, so here it is: a bright little colour screen that fits the dash, shows temperature, revs, fuel and battery, and talks to your phone.

What it does

An old van tells you almost nothing. This fills in the gaps, without making the dash look like it came out of a different car.

Shows what the engine is doing

Coolant temperature, revs, fuel consumption and whatever else the engine's own electronics know. It reads them from the diagnostic socket, so there are no extra sensors to fit for these.

Looks like it belongs

Deep black screen, readable in sunlight, with a background picture of your choice. It dims itself when you switch the ignition off and comes back when you start.

Phone app

The same numbers live on your phone, plus the settings: what goes on which screen, brightness, updates. Bluetooth only, nothing to sign up for and no internet needed in the van.

A clock, finally

Most of the time it is just a nice clock. It stays on with the engine off and is correct when you come back, which is more than the original ever managed.

Battery

Voltage and whether the alternator is charging. The sensor module measures right at the battery, so a tired battery shows up before it strands you somewhere.

Updates for free

I keep working on it, and every improvement reaches your display through the app. If an update ever fails, the display puts the old version back on its own.

Fitting it

An afternoon with normal tools. You do not cut into the engine wiring, and you can take it out again without a trace.

Mount the screen

It goes in the dash where the original clock or a blank panel sits. The cable runs behind the dash.

Plug in

Power and the van's diagnostic socket. The sensor module, if you have it, lives near the battery and connects with the plugs in the box.

Pair your phone

Install the app, connect, choose what you want on the screen. That is it.

Questions

Will it work with my van?

I made it for the VW T3 and it works with the engines most of them run today, including the usual swaps from the 1990s and 2000s. If you are not sure, email me what engine you have and I will tell you honestly. With the sensor module it is useful even on older engines with no diagnostic socket.

Do I need to be good with electronics?

No. Everything plugs in with the supplied connectors. If you can fit a car radio, you can fit this.

Does it need internet or a SIM card?

No. The display and your phone talk directly over Bluetooth. Updates download to your phone when it has internet and are passed on next time you are in the van.

Is there an iPhone app?

Android now. The iPhone app is in progress, and I will say so here when it is ready rather than promise a date.

Will it drain my battery?

No. With the engine off it draws about as much as a car clock.

How long does shipping take?

I ship within a few working days from Latvia. Within the EU expect 3 to 7 days, further away 1 to 3 weeks. You get a tracking number by email.

What if something goes wrong?

Every unit is tested before it ships, and there is a 2-year warranty. Most problems I can fix with an update through the app. A hardware fault gets repaired or replaced, no arguing.

Who is behind this?

One person, an electronics engineer in Latvia with a T3 and too many ideas. Ratronic is the name I put on the boards. If you write, it is me who answers.
