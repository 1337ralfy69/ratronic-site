Questions

Will it work with my van?

It is made for the VW T3 and works with the engines most of them have today, including common engine swaps from the 1990s and 2000s. Not sure? Email us with what engine you have and we will tell you straight away. With the sensor module it is useful even on older engines with no diagnostic socket.

Do I need to be good with electronics?

No. Everything plugs in with the supplied connectors. If you can fit a car radio, you can fit this.

Does it need internet or a SIM card?

No. The display and your phone talk directly over Bluetooth. Updates download to your phone when it has internet and are passed on next time you are in the van.

Is there an iPhone app?

The Android app is available now. The iPhone app is in progress.

Will it drain my battery?

No. With the engine off it uses about as much as a car clock.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within a few working days from Latvia. Within the EU expect 3 to 7 days, worldwide 1 to 3 weeks depending on the destination. You get a tracking number by email.

What if something goes wrong?

Every unit is tested before shipping and carries a 2-year warranty. Most things can be fixed with an update through the app; a hardware fault gets a repair or replacement.
